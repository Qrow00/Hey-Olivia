# Health & Wearables

## Supported Devices
- Smartwatches (WearOS, Android)
- Fitness bands
- Smart rings
- Medical devices

## Health Metrics
- Heart rate (BPM)
- Blood oxygen (SpO2)
- Steps count
- Sleep duration
- Calories burned
- Stress level
- Body temperature

## API Endpoints
- `/api/v1/wearables` — list/register devices
- `/api/v1/wearables/{id}/health` — record/get health data
- `/api/v1/wearables/{id}/health/history` — health history

## Voice Commands
- "What's my heart rate?"
- "How many steps today?"
- "How did I sleep?"
- "What's my blood oxygen?"
