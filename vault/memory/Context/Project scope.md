## What J.A.R.V.I.S. does
Voice-first personal AI assistant inspired by Iron Man's JARVIS. Listens via microphone, responds via TTS, controls devices, monitors system, browses web, manages routines.

## What it does NOT do
- Not a smart speaker replacement (no music streaming)
- Not a general-purpose chatbot (focused on assistance and automation)
- Not cloud-dependent (local-first)

## Key design principles
- Voice-first, keyboard-second
- Proactive, not just reactive
- Local-first, privacy-preserving
- Modular — services are swappable
